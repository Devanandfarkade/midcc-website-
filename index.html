STEP-BY-STEP FACTORY APPROVAL ROADMAP

Industrial Compliances & Legal Permits

What is this section for? It explains the 5 key government licenses, environmental consents, and safety approvals required to legally set up and operate a manufacturing plant in Kurkumbh MIDC.

STEP 01
🏛️
LAND ALLOTMENT

MIDC Plot Offer & Lease

Receive official plot allotment letter from MIDC Pune and register your industrial lease deed.

STEP 02
📜
ENVIRONMENTAL

MPCB CTE Clearance

Obtain "Consent to Establish" from MPCB prior to starting factory construction or chimney erection.

STEP 03
🏗️
FACTORY SAFETY

DISH Plan Approval

Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health approval for factory structural safety & worker welfare.

STEP 04
💧
INFRASTRUCTURE

CETP & Utility Linkage

Connect effluent pipeline to 1 MLD Kurkumbh CETP & secure MSEDCL high-voltage power grid load.

STEP 05
🚀
FINAL LICENSE

MPCB CTO Clearance

Obtain "Consent to Operate" after final MPCB inspection to commence commercial manufacturing.
